About SmartGrid

One connected platform. Smarter school management.

Managing a school involves much more than classrooms and lessons. It requires coordination, accurate information, effective communication and informed decision-making.

SmartGrid is being developed to simplify these responsibilities through an integrated digital school management platform.

Our vision is to make essential school information and administrative processes more accessible, organised and connected.

The SmartGrid Platform

Designed around the needs of modern schools.

SmartGrid brings together important areas of school administration and educational management.

01 / MANAGEMENT

School Administration

Organise essential school information, administrative activities and institutional operations through a connected platform.

02 / LEARNERS

Learner Management

Support the organisation and management of learner information throughout the educational journey.

03 / LEARNER ATTENDANCE

Learner Attendance Management

Simplify daily attendance recording and monitoring. Maintain accurate attendance records, monitor absenteeism and keep parents informed about important attendance updates.

04 / STAFF ATTENDANCE

Staff Attendance Management

Simplify staff attendance recording and monitoring. Maintain accurate staff attendance records, monitor punctuality and strengthen accountability across the institution.

05 / EXAMINATIONS

Examinations and Assessment Management

Manage examinations, assessments and learner performance in one organised environment. Record marks, manage assessment records, track academic progress and generate performance reports.

06 / ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE

Academic Performance and Reporting

Turn academic records into meaningful information. Monitor learner progress and generate reports that support teachers, school administrators and informed decision-making.

07 / COMMUNICATION

Parent Communication

Strengthen communication between schools and parents through accessible information and important notifications.

08 / INSIGHTS

Information and Reporting

Support better administrative oversight through organised school information and reporting capabilities.
